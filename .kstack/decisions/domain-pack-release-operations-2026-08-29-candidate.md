# Release-operations domain pack candidate

**Candidate:** `release-operations@1.0.0`
**Authority:** analysis and evidence requirements only; inactive

The candidate has four ordered sections: readiness, observation, rollback, and
handoff. It requires immutable candidate/dependency/readiness evidence,
digest-bound health baselines and windows, measurable rollback eligibility and
safety evidence, and an ordered action/observation/decision/owner receipt
ledger. Missing authority evidence remains `unknown`; the pack cannot deploy,
roll back, call Jira, select itself, suppress a base lane, or grant a tool.

The checked-in canonical D5 bundle is independently digest-bound and is below
the D6 hard byte cap. It remains unavailable until the exact candidate clears
the frozen D7 300-case A/B/C trial, D4/D10 evidence fixtures, D6 provider
budget qualification, two-person adjudication, independent final review, and a
separate owner activation through D5. Qualification is not activation.

The original frozen v1 release-operations profile remains supported, and the
candidate is also bound to the common v2 multi-pack campaign route. Neither
route contains authentic trial output or an activation decision.
