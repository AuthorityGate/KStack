# Research-knowledge domain pack candidate

**Candidate:** `research-knowledge@1.0.0`
**Authority:** analysis and evidence requirements only; inactive

The candidate has four ordered sections: question/scope, source quality,
synthesis, and decision use. It requires explicit claim and stopping
boundaries, tested assumptions, primary/current/traceable sources, missing
perspectives and counterevidence, labeled inference and uncertainty, preserved
source conflict, conclusion limits, and exact reproducible citation support.
It cannot browse, choose sources, select itself, turn citations into
instructions, suppress a base lane, or grant a tool.

The checked-in canonical D5 bundle is independently digest-bound and below the
D6 hard byte cap. It needs its own held-out research corpus across source
quality, contradiction, currency, synthesis, and citation strata; independent
natural-person adjudication; baseline noninferiority; case-level unsupported
claim bounds; D4/D10 evidence and D6 budget gates; independent final review;
and a separate owner D5 activation after qualification.

Its v2 evaluation profile is now executable and digest-bound across
citation/decision use, contradiction/counterevidence, question/scope, source
quality, and synthesis/inference. This supplies the campaign contract only;
no authentic cases or qualification result are claimed.
